Professional knowledge

Buying guides that save research time

Short checklists covering technical questions and common sourcing mistakes.

Buying guide

OEM appliance RFQ checklist

Move beyond generic photos toward an auditable product specification.

  • State model, quantity, packaging and destination market.
  • Request exact test report model and certificate scope.
  • Clarify tooling ownership, warranty and spare-parts support.
Buying guide

Voltage and plug requirements

A plug adapter alone does not make every appliance suitable.

  • Check both voltage and frequency ratings.
  • Identify the required plug and protective earthing design.
  • Confirm local conformity requirements before sale.
Buying guide

Compare sampling and inspection

Agree on tolerances and acceptance rules before paying for bulk production.

  • Define sample sign-off and any golden sample.
  • List safety-critical inspection and functional tests.
  • Specify third-party inspection and rejection rules in the contract.

Frequently asked questions

Buy with more confidence

Answers explain what this information can and cannot tell you.

Can every appliance work worldwide?

No. Voltage, frequency, safety certification, plug and labeling requirements differ by market.

Can I place an OEM order here?

Not yet. This is an independent reference website prototype.

Does PENG certify factories?

No. Any future verification activity must describe who checked what, when and by which method.

Transparency: Product information is educational and does not establish electrical safety certification, importer obligations or approval to sell. This website is an early knowledge prototype. It is not a trading company, payment intermediary or product certification body.
